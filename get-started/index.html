Get started

Up and running in minutes.

Allow about 10–15 minutes on a computer the first time. You make a copy, run Get ready and allow the script, then do a short publish step. After that, the app is yours on your phone.

Before you start

What you'll need

👤A Google account
☁️Some free Drive space
💻A computer, once, for setup
📱Your phone (Android or iPhone)

A computer, the first time. The phone can't make the Google copy, tick the permission screen or publish the web app for you. Plan for about 10–15 minutes at a desk, then everything else is on your phone.

Four steps

From copy to collecting.

  1. Copy the template

    On a computer, open the Make a copy link (it ends in /copy), or use File → Make a copy. Your rows and photos live in your Google account from then on.

  2. Get ready and allow it

    From the sheet menu, run “1. Get ready (run once)” and allow access. It makes your photo, drop-zone and export folders. Google’s unverified-app warning is normal — choose Advanced, then Allow.

  3. Publish the web app

    This is a short, separate step, not automatic. In the script editor choose Deploy → New deployment → Web app → Deploy (about five clicks). Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone. The in-app guide and the PDF walk through each click.

  4. Open your phone link

    After you deploy, use “2. Show my phone app link” (the link and a QR code are also in the Settings tab). Open it on your phone, tap Yes, connect, add it to your home screen, and follow the wizard.

The publish step is part of every setup, not a fallback. The step-by-step setup and the PDF guide show each Deploy click. Collecson isn't on sale yet, so there's no public template link.

Setup questions

Good to know.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes. One owner needs a Google account to make a copy of the template and deploy its included script. Family members can use the private phone link without signing in to Google. Some work or school accounts block the required deployment option; a personal account is usually simplest.

Where does my data live? Is it private?

Collection rows are in your copy of Google Sheets; photos, backgrounds and exports are in your Google Drive. The app’s interface is a shared static website, not a central collection database. However, the deployed web app runs as you with access set to “Anyone”: treat its URL and phone link as private keys. Anyone with the link can see non-money collection details and may edit them. The owner PIN hides money; it is not a full sign-in or permission system.

Does Google Drive cost money?

A personal Google account normally includes 15 GB at no charge, shared between Drive, Gmail and Google Photos. Your photos use that allowance. If you run out, reduce photo sizes, clear space or choose a paid Google storage plan. Storage prices vary by account and country. Google’s Apps Script usage limits also apply.

Why does Google say “hasn’t verified this app”?

You are authorising your own copy of the included script, not a Google-reviewed commercial app. Google can show an unverified-app warning. Review the script and permissions first. The guide describes “Advanced → Go to [your project] (unsafe) → Allow” only for your own trusted copy. Don’t bypass a warning for an unfamiliar app. If your account blocks access, stop rather than weaken its security settings.

Can I add my own details?

Yes. On any item tap ＋ Add field: text, number, date, tick, money (private to you), a photo, or a dated log such as a Service log or Repairs (date, note, cost and photos). Fields apply to the whole collection and become columns in your sheet (logs go in a Logs tab). You can rename or remove them; removing only hides a field, the data stays in your sheet. Only the owner can add or change fields; family can fill them in.

Do I have to add other people?

No. It’s set up for just you. If you want, add Family (can browse, add items and photos, never sees money) or a Kid (Kid mode: big buttons, points, badges and jobs, no prices; their photos wait for your OK, and leaving Kid mode needs your PIN). Anyone holding the private app link can choose a non-owner name, so keep it private. Only the money view and settings require the PIN.

Does it work without the internet?

Partly. The app reopens with what it last loaded, and changes you make with no signal are saved on the phone and sent when you’re back online. Setup, new data from the sheet and photo uploads need an internet connection. Keep your own backups of the Sheet and Drive photos.

What will it cost?

Two one-off options, paid once: Collecson at £10 and Collecson Family at £15. Nothing is on sale yet, and there is no subscription. See the Pricing page.
