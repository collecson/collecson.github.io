Features

Everything a collector needs.

Collecson does the everyday jobs brilliantly and leaves room for the details only your hobby has. Screens shown use invented example collections.

Do I own it?

Never buy it twice.

At a fair, car boot or shop, type a name (typos are fine) or scan the barcode. Collecson checks your whole collection in a second and shows the match with its photo.

  • Fuzzy search that forgives typos
  • Barcode scanning with the phone camera
  • Spares and duplicates easy to spot and mark for sale

Your own fields

Make it fit your hobby.

Tap ＋ Add field on any item. Pick a type and it appears on every item in the collection, and as a new column in your sheet.

  • Text, number, date, tick, money (private), photo
  • Dated logs, e.g. Service log, Tasting log, Care log, with notes, costs and photos
  • Rename or remove any time; removing hides it and keeps the data

Value

Know what it’s worth.

What you paid, what it’s worth, the difference and this month’s change. Money only appears after the owner unlocks with a 4-digit PIN.

  • Collection value and spend on Stats
  • Per-item value with where the figure came from
  • Keep me unlocked on this phone for 30 days (optional)

Wanted & shop links

Keep the hunt going.

A Wanted list with max prices and notes, plus set checklists to track the gaps. One tap searches the usual places.

  • eBay UK (live and sold), Amazon UK, Vinted, Facebook Marketplace, Google Shopping
  • Your own saved shop link per wanted item
  • Got it? Move it into the collection in one tap

Photos

Every angle, safely filed.

Front, back and detail shots from the camera or gallery, or short videos. Each item gets its own folder in your Google Drive.

  • Set the main photo for the grid
  • Photos-to-take list so nothing gets missed
  • Share-a-find cards with no prices on them

Family & Kid mode

Just you, or the whole household.

It’s set up for just you. If you like, add Family (browse, add items and photos, never sees money) or a Kid.

  • Kid mode: big buttons, points, badges and jobs, no prices
  • Kids’ photos wait for your OK
  • Leaving Kid mode needs your PIN

Help built in

Shown round, every screen.

A two-minute walkthrough runs after setup and visits every screen. Skip it any time, replay it from Settings or Help.

  • An I’m stuck button on every screen
  • Five-step setup wizard for name, colours and categories
  • A beginner setup guide (PDF) with every click

Straight talk

Honest about the edges.

✓ What it is

  • A phone-friendly catalogue for any collection
  • Connected to your own copy of a Google Sheet, with photos in your Drive
  • A home-screen web app you install from your link
  • Works offline with what it last loaded; changes sync when you’re back online

✗ What it isn’t (yet)

  • Not on Google Play or the App Store
  • Not a valuation service: values are yours to set
  • Not a full sign-in system: the PIN hides money, so keep your link private
  • Not on sale yet: nothing to buy on this site
