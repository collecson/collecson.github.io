The usual questions

Good to know.

Is it a one-off payment?

Yes. You pay once for the product you choose. There's no monthly or yearly subscription. Support terms and how updates are included are still being written.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes. Collecson is a phone app on top of a Google Sheet in your own account. Photos and PDFs are stored in your Google Drive. You don't create an account with us.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It's a home-screen web app: add it from your private link in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). It isn't on the App Store or Google Play.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. If you start with Collecson, you'll be able to add Family later for the difference (that would be £5 at these prices). Kids mode stays hidden until Family is on.

Where does my data live?

In your Google account: the sheet, plus Drive folders for photos and documents. Nothing is shared from your account, and this site has no analytics or tracking.
