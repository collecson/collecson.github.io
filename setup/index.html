Setup, step by step

Every click, from copy to Home.

Honest version: the first time takes a computer, and it isn't fully automatic. You make a copy, run Get ready and allow the script, then do a short publish step (Deploy → New deployment → Web app → Deploy). The phone wizard is the easy part.

1. Copy the template2. Get ready and allow it3. Publish the web app4. Your phone link5. The setup wizardAdding a kid (Family)If something goes wrong

Step 1

Copy the template

When Collecson is on sale, you'll get a Make a copy link (it ends in /copy). On a computer, signed into the Google account you want to use, open that link, or choose File → Make a copy, and save it in your Drive. That copy is your collection: rows in the sheet, photos and PDFs in Drive folders next to it.

Use a computer for this. A phone can open the sheet, but Google's copy step is much less fiddly at a desk.

Step 2

Get ready, and allow the script

In your copy, open the ★ Collecson menu and choose 1. Get ready (run once) and allow access. It makes your photo, drop-zone and export folders. Google will show a permission screen — that's expected, because the script needs to write to your sheet and Drive, not anyone else's.

  • Google’s unverified-app warning is normal. Choose Advanced, then Allow (or Go to Collecson). You are allowing your copy, not a public app of ours.
  • Get ready sets up the folders and permissions. It does not publish the phone app for you — that's the next step.

Step 3

Publish the web app

This is a short, separate step, about five clicks. The in-app guide and the PDF walk through each one. You need to do this on a computer.

  • Open the included Apps Script (from the Collecson menu or Extensions → Apps Script).
  • Choose Deploy → New deployment, set the type to Web app, then click Deploy.
  • Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. (Your data still lives in your account; this only lets your phone reach the script.)
  • Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec) back into the sheet if you're asked, or continue to Show my phone app link.

The beginner setup guide (PDF) shows each of these screens. Open the setup guide (PDF, 839 KB)

Step 4

Open your phone link

After you deploy, use 2. Show my phone app link. You'll get the link and a QR code (they're also in the Settings tab). Send the link to your phone, or scan the QR. You'll see Connect this phone? Tap Yes, connect only if it's your link.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link in Chrome and tap Yes, connect.
  2. Tap ⋮ top right → Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. The Collecson icon sits with your other apps.

🍏 iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the link in Safari and tap Yes, connect.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. It opens full-screen, like any other app.

Use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone, not the browser inside Mail, Facebook or WhatsApp.

Step 5

The setup wizard, screen by screen

It takes a couple of minutes. The numbered steps in the app are: name and type, look, categories, you and your PIN, then a quick check. Here is every screen you'll actually see, including the intro and your first Home. Then snap a photo, add your own fields with ＋ Add field, and let the two-minute tour show you round.

Family

Adding a kid later.

The wizard is set up for just you. If you have Collecson Family, you can add a family member or a kid during setup, or any time in More → Settings → Change name, type, colours, people.

  • Family can add items and photos, and never sees money.
  • A Kid gets Kid mode: big buttons, points, badges, jobs and rewards, no prices.
  • Photos a child adds wait for your OK. Leaving Kid mode needs your PIN.

On the solo Collecson product, Kids mode stays hidden until you add Family. See Pricing.

Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong.

Google says this app hasn't been verified

That's normal for a script that lives in your own copy of the sheet. On the warning screen, open Advanced, then Go to Collecson (or the project name), and allow it. You're allowing your copy, not a public app.

I can't find Get ready, or the Collecson menu

The Collecson menu appears after you open your copy of the sheet on a computer. Refresh the sheet if you've just made the copy. Get ready sets up your folders; it does not publish the phone app. For that, follow Publish the web app above, or the clicks in the PDF guide.

I'm stuck on the Deploy step

Open Extensions → Apps Script, then Deploy → New deployment → Web app → Deploy. Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec). The in-app guide and the PDF show each screen. If Google asks you to turn on the Apps Script API, you can ignore that switch — it isn't needed for this setup.

The phone link won't connect

Check it ends in /exec, and that the web app was deployed with “Who has access: Anyone” (the data still lives in your account; this only lets your phone reach the script). Don't open the link inside Mail, Facebook or WhatsApp — use Chrome or Safari. After you deploy, use “2. Show my phone app link” or the Settings tab for the link and QR code.

The app looks old, or the wizard doesn't appear

Close it fully and reopen it, or pull down to refresh. If you already finished setup, you'll go straight to the app; you can change the name, type, colours and people from Settings.

I want to start again with the examples

Remove example items from Home or Settings (it only touches the samples). If you change the collection type before you've added real items, the examples can swap to match.
